# Design references — Strompilot Leitstand

Short list of similar tools and visual references for the redesign
direction. The owner picks one or two to aim at.

## Closest to our idea

- [Tibber](https://tibber.com/en) — dynamic spot pricing app with smart
  EV charging and automation, our nearest competitor.
- [Ostrom](https://ostrom.de) — German green power provider with
  dynamic tariffs and a clean consumer app.
- [Octopus Energy](https://www.octopus.energy) — dynamic tariffs with
  famously good consumer UX and price displays.
- [EVCC](https://evcc.io) — open-source German solar and EV charging
  dashboard, hacker-grade density.
- [aWATTar](https://www.awattar.com) — hourly energy marketplace, the
  price source we already use.

## Best visual references for the control room feel

- [Electricity Maps](https://www.electricitymaps.com) — beautiful
  real-time energy flow and price visualization.
- [Home Assistant energy dashboard](https://www.home-assistant.io) —
  the gold standard open-source home energy view.
- [Energy Charts](https://energy-charts.info) — Fraunhofer price and
  grid charts, dense and honest.
